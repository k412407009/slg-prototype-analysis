## Key Recalibration

方向 A：港口开箱 dropped from 78.5 to 70.0 because the old score over-rewarded D0 attraction. Its front-end audience is more RNG / collecting / simulation-management oriented, and the current SLG conversion path requires proof that those users can move into merchant guild, route control, alliance bidding, or map competition without major leakage.

Top War remains 77.0 as a separate object because the front-end merge loop already uses military units, base upgrades, and battle power as the understandable reward language. Its casual entry is not identical to hard SLG, but the bridge toward unit upgrades, alliance war, and map competition is comparatively direct.

Top Heroes is separated at 73.5 because its red-dragon / vertical one-finger RPG entry attracts hero-exploration and character-growth users. Those users can accept squad power checks, but hard map war or alliance pressure needs an extra conversion bridge, so it should not share Top War's score.

方向 E：TCG 卡店帝国 also dropped for the same reason: pack opening and collecting can be very strong, but card-shop users are not automatically alliance-war users.

方向 J：防疫区 and Kingshot hold stronger positions because screening, tower defense, governance, rebellion, and city defense already teach users to accept threat pressure and battle validation.
