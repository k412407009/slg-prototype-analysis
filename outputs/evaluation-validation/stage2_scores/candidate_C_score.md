## 4. Verdict

Score: 71.5/100. Decision band: Small-sample validation.

The pitch has a strong identity and D0 hook, but the Stage 1 material is less solid on backend proof, source consistency, and sensitivity risk. It should not be greenlit beyond a limited validation package until those gaps are resolved.

## 5. Main Risks

- Disease, patients, plague, and rat imagery may create creative-platform or regional sensitivity issues.
- The "doctor saving people" identity could fracture if D7+ becomes ordinary war without strong medical-resource logic.
- Source attribution and system descriptions are inconsistent enough to reduce confidence.
- Long-term monetization depth is plausible but not demonstrated by the material.

## 6. What Would Need Validation First

- Confirm actual early flow: hospital construction, herb production, patient rescue, rat defense, and first exploration.
- Verify whether the D7+ backend has real alliance map, territory, troop, loss, and resource-control systems.
- Test ad concepts for policy sensitivity and expectation mismatch, especially plague doctor cinematic versus actual gameplay.
