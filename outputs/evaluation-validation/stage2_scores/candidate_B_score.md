## 4. Verdict

Score: 80/100. Decision band: Can enter project validation.

The material supports a strong lightweight operating-game pitch with a plausible sea-SLG backend. It reaches the threshold mainly through front-end clarity and UA specificity; backend depth still needs proof before larger commitment.

## 5. Main Risks

- Management users attracted by raft growth may reject timed alliance warfare.
- If the backend stays too soft, long-term SLG monetization depth may be insufficient.
- If it becomes too heavy, the light raft-management promise may become misleading.
- Exact production-center vulnerability and war-loop rules are not established.

## 6. What Would Need Validation First

- D0-D3 playable flow for salvage, fish production, resident orders, and automation.
- D7+ transition test from raft management into fleet/cargo/alliance-city objectives.
- Confirmation of personal production attack rules, fleet durability, fuel, repair, and season structure.
