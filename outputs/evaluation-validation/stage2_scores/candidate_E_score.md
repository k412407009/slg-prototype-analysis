## 4. Verdict

Score: 77/100. Decision band: Small-sample validation.

The material gives a credible medieval-governance variant of shelter-to-4X design, but the Stage 1 evidence depends on backward inference and leaves key early-flow details unconfirmed. It merits focused validation rather than full greenlight.

## 5. Main Risks

- It may feel structurally too close to existing shelter-survival formulas unless law, labor, rebellion, and kingship are strongly differentiated.
- Tower-defense and rescue creatives may acquire users who reject later alliance 4X pressure.
- Early version flow, migration, casualty, formation, and kingdom activity rules need confirmation.

## 6. What Would Need Validation First

- D0-D3 playable test for refugee rescue, worker assignment, food pressure, law choice, and first rebel defense.
- D7+ transition test into alliance help, strategic buildings, kingdom events, migration, and throne goals.
- Creative fidelity test for rescue, tower defense, law choices, and actual first-day gameplay.
