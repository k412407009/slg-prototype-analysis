## 4. Verdict

Score: 83.5/100. Decision band: Can enter project validation.

This is the strongest Stage 1 pitch material because the front-end emotion, early loop, and SLG backend are connected by one survival logic. The score is not a judgment of market success; it reflects that the material gives enough reviewable structure to justify validation.

## 5. Main Risks

- The ad-facing furnace survival loop may over-attract users who do not want D7+ alliance warfare.
- Early functionality, soft-launch creative, D0-D30 unlock order, migration, burn, casualty, and state-event rules remain unverified.
- Ice/survival visual fatigue and theme sameness could weaken UA differentiation.

## 6. What Would Need Validation First

- Playable D0-D7 build showing furnace rescue, resident simulation, first hero squad, and alliance help timing.
- D7-D30 rules for alliance map, losses, relocation, fort/castle control, and state events.
- Creative-to-build consistency tests for furnace rescue, clearing snow, and shelter recovery ads.
